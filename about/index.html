    FAQs

    Still have questions?
    We got your back!

    Does Remeco sell products on this website?

    No. This site is a catalogue of the Remeco range. You can buy Remeco products from pharmacies and online stores. The Where to buy page lists the stores we have found selling them, with links.

    Which Pepticon is right for me?

    All three Pepticon suspensions relieve heartburn and acid indigestion. Pepticon Liquid forms a protective layer on top of the stomach contents. Pepticon Double Action also neutralises excess stomach acid. Pepticon Advance forms a strong, resilient barrier and is ideal for more severe or persistent reflux symptoms. Ask your pharmacist which one suits you.

    What sizes does Pepticon come in?

    Pepticon Double Action, Pepticon Liquid and Pepticon Advance each come in 100ml and 200ml packs.

    Is Proladii suitable for children?

    Proladii contains Saccharomyces boulardii, an evidence-based probiotic with a proven excellent safety profile for adults & children. It comes as 250mg capsules and as single-serving powder sachets.

    Are Remmis gummies gelatin-free?

    Yes. Remmis gummies contain no gelatin, gluten or preservatives. Remmis Elderberry, Biotin, Apple Cider Vinegar and Multivitamin gummies are vegan-friendly.

    Which Remmis gummies are made for children?

    Remmis DHA Gummies and Remmis Multivitamin Gummies are sugar-free, Halal-certified and specially formulated for children aged 2 years & above.
